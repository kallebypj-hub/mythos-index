Mythos Index

Every god, monster, hero and weapon of world mythology, explained one chapter at a time.

Mythos Index is a YouTube channel that publishes long-form explainers built from classical sources and public-domain art. Watch on YouTube.

This site also hosts the information required for the channel's publishing tool, an internal application that uploads the channel's own videos through the official YouTube Data API.
