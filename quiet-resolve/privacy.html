Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Who we are

Quiet Resolve is a YouTube channel. Its publishing tool ("the app") is an internal application used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos.

What the app accesses

The app uses Google OAuth to request access to the channel owner's YouTube account with these scopes: uploading videos (youtube.upload) and reading the channel's own videos and statistics (youtube.readonly). It is not offered to the public and no other person's account is accessed.

How the data is used

  • To upload videos produced by the channel, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and scheduled publishing time.
  • To read the channel's own video statistics in order to plan future videos.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the channel owner's private server. Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
